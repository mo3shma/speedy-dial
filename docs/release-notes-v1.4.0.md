## Speedy Dial 1.4.0

The first public release.

**What is in it**
- A dial grid that always fits the screen, with drag to reorder and a right-click menu
- A history sidebar on the right edge, with a pin
- Optional widgets on the left edge: clock, calendar, notes and to-do
- High-resolution icons found automatically, with a Find icon search by name
- Two looks, Liquid glass and Material 3 Expressive, plus light, dark and auto themes and your own background photo
- One-click add from the toolbar button, `Alt+Shift+D` or the right-click menu
- A seven-step welcome tour on a fresh install
- Export and import of your dials

**Install:** download `speedy-dial-v1.4.0.zip`, unzip it somewhere you will keep it, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the folder. Full steps are in the [README](../README.md#install).

**Also attached:** `speedy-dial-launch.mp4`, the 50-second launch video (1920x1080).
