## Speedy Dial 1.5.0

**Folders, lite mode, and import from another speed dial.**

- Folders: the + button now asks whether to add a dial or a folder. A folder shows a small grid of its sites and opens in a popup, where you can rename it and add dials. Drag dials in and out, or right-click for Move to folder, Move out, Ungroup and Delete folder.
- Lite mode: if hardware acceleration looks off or the page runs slowly, Speedy Dial flattens the glass effects and animations by itself and shows a banner that can open your browser's graphics settings. Settings > Graphics has Auto, Full effects and Lite.
- Import: Settings > Backup and tips reads a bookmarks HTML file, a JSON backup (folders stay folders) or a plain list of addresses. It shows what it found, skips sites you already have, and never deletes anything.
- Backups now include folders.

**Install:** download `speedy-dial-v1.5.0.zip`, unzip it somewhere you will keep it, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the folder. Full steps are in the [README](../README.md#install).
