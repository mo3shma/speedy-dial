## Speedy Dial 1.4.1

**Access to all sites is now optional.**

- Speedy Dial no longer asks for access to every site at install. It asks once, from a new "Sharp icons" step in the welcome tour, from a switch in Settings, or when you refresh or search for an icon.
- Without it you still get icons from public icon services and Chrome's favicon cache. With it, Speedy Dial reads the sites you add, only to fetch their full-size icons.
- When you allow it, existing dials re-fetch their icons at full size. Icons you picked yourself are kept.
- The welcome tour is now eight steps.

**Install:** download `speedy-dial-v1.4.1.zip`, unzip it somewhere you will keep it, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the folder. Full steps are in the [README](../README.md#install).
