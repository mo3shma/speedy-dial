# Privacy Policy for Speedy Dial

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Speedy Dial is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with a grid of your favorite sites. It is made by 3shmaaa.

## The short version

Speedy Dial has no server, no account and no analytics. Nothing about you is sent to the author or to any third party that the author controls. Everything it stores stays on your device.

## What is stored, and where

Your dials, layout, theme, widget content (notes, to-dos), background image and settings are stored in the browser's local extension storage on your device. Removing the extension deletes them. You can also export them to a file yourself and import them later.

## What the extension reads

- **Browsing history and most-visited sites.** Read through the browser's `history` and `topSites` permissions, only to show the history sidebar and suggestions on your own screen. This data is not stored by Speedy Dial beyond what the browser already holds, and it is never transmitted.
- **The page you are on.** When you click the toolbar button, press Alt+Shift+D or use the "Add to Speedy Dial" menu item, the extension reads that page's address and title to create a dial. It does not read page content and does not run code in the pages you visit.

## Network requests

To build sharp dial icons, the extension requests icon files and web app manifests from the sites you add, and looks them up on public icon services (for example Google's favicon service, icon.horse, Iconify and Wikimedia Commons). Those requests contain the site's domain or your search words, as any web request would, and are subject to those services' own privacy policies.

If you choose to add your own Google API key and search engine ID in Settings, your icon searches are sent to Google's Custom Search API. The key is stored only on your device.

## What Speedy Dial does not do

- It does not sell or share your data.
- It does not use your data for advertising, profiling or credit decisions.
- It does not use remote code. All code is packaged in the extension.
- It does not track you across sites.

## Children

Speedy Dial is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date.
